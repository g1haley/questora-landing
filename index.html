Straight answers

Questions before you jump in.

Do I need to know how to code? +
No. Build Mode drafts the adventure from your description, and the editors are all forms — rooms, items, characters, puzzles. If you can describe a place and edit a paragraph, you can build one.
Does the AI just make the whole game for me? +
No — and that's the honest pitch. Build Mode gives you a strong first draft fast: rooms, items, characters, starter dialogue. You then connect, tune and polish it into a finished adventure. Think collaborator, not vending machine — and the built-in Solver checks that your result is actually completable.
Are the characters "smart"? +
The story and hints are authored by whoever built the adventure. On top of that, you can free-chat with characters and your local model improvises in-character replies. They're conversational flavor — not puzzle-solving agents that track the whole game state.
What do I need to run the AI? +
A regular desktop or laptop. Questora runs small local models (a 1–3B GGUF model works well) on CPU, and faster if you have a GPU. You download a model once and never pay per use. Nothing is sent to the cloud.
Is the free player really free — or a trial? +
Genuinely free and unlimited. It's the full playing experience with the bundled adventures. Creator is the separate one-time unlock that adds the authoring tools.
When can I actually buy Creator? +
Honest answer: Questora Creator is in active development and not on sale yet. This page is how I gauge who wants it — join the launch list and you'll get first access, plus a founding-creator discount when it ships. No charge today.
Subscription? Ongoing fees? +
None. $12.99 is a one-time unlock. You own the version you buy, it runs offline forever, and updates during launch are included.
